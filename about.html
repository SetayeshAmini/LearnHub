— HOW IT STARTED

A shared history before
LearnHub existed

01
The Beginning

Alex was hiring. James applied. They met through a job posting at Alex's company. What started as a working relationship quickly became a real collaboration — building projects together, learning how the other thinks.

02
The Turning Point

An international EdTech startup — and a platform built from scratch. Alex was invited to join. They brought James. Together they built a full online learning platform — from zero. That experience became the foundation.

03
The Decision

Time to build something of their own. After years of experience, they had the vision and a clear problem to solve: aspiring developers needed a real technology partner, not another platform to figure out alone. LearnHub was born.

04
Today

Two founders, real partnerships, live students. LearnHub is bootstrapped, growing, and built around a simple belief: the best learning technology is invisible — and the best support feels like having a mentor who handles the technical side so you can focus on coding.

"The best technology is invisible — and the best support feels like having a co-founder who handles the technical side so you can focus on learning."
